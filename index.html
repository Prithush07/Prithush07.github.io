Research logic shown in the dashboard.

  • Market state: rolling Ledoit-Wolf correlation matrices.
  • Physics bridge: correlations treated as financial spin-like configurations.
  • Classical observables: average correlation, top eigenvalue, cross-sectional volatility, Binder-like cumulant.

  • Residualisation: learned score after removing classical controls.
  • Trading rule: lagged exposure, volatility targeting, turnover fees.
  • Validation: forward realised volatility used only as an evaluation target.
